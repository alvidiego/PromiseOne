# Architecture Decision Records

This folder captures major PromiseOne architecture and design decisions.

Use these records to preserve why important choices were made, not just what changed.
Keep each ADR short, practical, and easy for a future Codex conversation to scan.

After accepting or updating an ADR, review `docs/CURRENT_WORK.md` and update any
active direction, experiment, or open question affected by the decision.

## Naming

Use numbered, lowercase, dash-separated filenames:

```text
0001-short-decision-name.md
0002-short-decision-name.md
```

## Template

```md
# ADR 0000: Decision Title

Status: Accepted
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
What problem, constraint, or question led to this decision?

## Decision
What did we decide?

## Why
Why is this the best choice right now?

## Alternatives Considered
What other options were considered?

## Consequences
What becomes easier?
What becomes harder?
What should future work remember?

## Related Files
- path/to/file
```
