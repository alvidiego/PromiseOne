What a real visit page would need

Before publication, a real organization would confirm its public address, monitored contact owner, current hours, closure process, transit and parking information, physical access, sensory conditions, restroom access, service-animal policy, language support, and emergency contact boundary.

A map alone would not establish those facts. This simulation therefore uses no map, external API, live status, directions provider, or location claim.
